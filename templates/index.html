Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our frames, ordering process, delivery, and returns.

What are the components of a frame?

Each frame includes a frame border, a glass panel cut to the required size, and an MDF backing that holds your customised photo in place.

How can I order a frame?

Browse the frames on our website and click Add to Bag on your desired frame. Proceed to checkout to contact us on WhatsApp. Share your photos, discuss the price, and pay the required advance. Once your order is confirmed, your frame will be delivered to your home within our service area.

Is home delivery available?

Yes! We currently offer home delivery within Raxaul. We hope to expand our delivery service to more locations in the future.

Do you have a return policy?

As our frames are customised according to each customer's requirements, returns are generally not accepted. Please confirm your photo and frame details carefully before placing your order.
